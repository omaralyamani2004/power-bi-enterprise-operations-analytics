# Business Insights

## Commercial concentration and margin risk

The unfiltered Commercial Sales view reports `$80.45M` in revenue and `$79.98M` in COGS, leaving a displayed gross profit margin of `0.58%`. Bikes contribute `$66.30M`, approximately `82.4%` of revenue, but the displayed category margin is `-1.49%`. The executive implication is clear: the largest revenue stream is also the main profitability-risk area, so pricing, discounting, product cost, and mix should be reviewed before interpreting volume as healthy growth.

## Regional prioritization

The dashboard's current insight table identifies Southwest as the strongest market at approximately `$18.5M`, while Australia is the weakest. A commercial team could use this view to compare the Southwest playbook with weaker markets and investigate whether the gap is driven by product mix, customer coverage, pricing, or fulfillment.

## Customer engagement snapshot

With 2013 selected, Customer Analysis reports `12.48K` customers, `1.17` average orders per customer, `$25M` in total sales, and approximately `1K` returning customers. Because the displayed values are rounded, the returning-customer share is approximately 8% rather than an exact rate. This suggests a practical next step: segment one-time buyers from repeat customers and test retention actions by country, income, education, and marital status.

## Responsible use of the insight

These insights are based on Adventure Works training data and are intended to demonstrate analytical reasoning. They are not claims about a real company's performance. Customer-level detail should only be used after the table measure is validated against the total and filtered customer context.
